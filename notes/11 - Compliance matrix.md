## B. Judging criteria (local and global)
| Criterion | Our evidence |
|---|---|
| **Impact** | Audience: students who never see a whole mission (the challenge's own words). Co-op mode for the classroom, 4 students per PC. Free, runs in the browser |
| **Creativity** | A grid with adjacency (heat, interference, contamination) is new in mission design games; operations by sols with communication delay |
| **Validity** | Playable build with a link; numbers with real anchors and a "simplified" label; rules based on real engineering |
| **Relevance** | Covers every item on the challenge's list (table A) and uses 5+ official Resources structurally |
| **Presentation** | Anchor sentence, 30 s demo with adjacency + event + report, 7 slides problem → solution → data → next step |
